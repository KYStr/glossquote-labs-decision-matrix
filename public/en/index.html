The formula

Benefit u = score − 1; cost u = 5 − score. Numerator N = Σ(weight × u), total weight W = Σ weight, and percentage score = 25 × N ÷ W. Zero weights do not contribute; a zero total cannot be ranked.

Ranking compares integer N. Ties keep the original option order with ranks such as 1, 1, 3. Displayed scores have two decimal places. This model organizes subjective trade-offs; it does not decide for you.

Frequently asked questions

Must weights add up to 100?

No. Weights are divided by their total. Zero-weight criteria do not contribute.

How do I score costs?

Score the cost or burden: 1 low, 5 high. Calculation reverses this so lower is more favorable.

What if some scores are missing?

Any missing score for a positive-weight criterion hides all totals and ranks.

Is the top-ranked option always right for me?

The ranking follows your criteria, weights and scores. It organizes trade-offs and cannot replace your judgment about the situation.

Can I save and continue later?

Download a JSON comparison file, then open it later. This tool does not automatically save or sync.
